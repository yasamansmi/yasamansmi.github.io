WorkWhile

What I work on

Worker incentives

Staffing needs change throughout the day, and incentives have to stay within budget. At WorkWhile, I built a system that updates those incentives every hour across thousands of shifts.

It uses Bayesian reinforcement learning and improved fill rate while reducing spend per requested position by 17%. My work also covered the training pipelines, online updates, and monitoring needed to run it in production.

Planning backup staffing

Backup staffing helps cover gaps, but it comes at a cost. I built understaffing forecasts and an optimizer that weighs staffing needs, replacement risk, and cost. The forecasts reduced validation mean absolute error by 20%. In an offline comparison, the optimizer recommended about 31% less backup staffing.

Experiments were another part of this work. In a separate staffing experiment, more shifts were filled, but net revenue fell. I presented the results and recommended against a broader rollout.

Bringing customer information together

Customer calls and meeting notes tell a different part of the story from marketplace data. I built an LLM-assisted workflow that brings those sources together to help account teams identify retention risks, expansion opportunities, and accounts that need more support.

I worked with account managers and Operations to validate the outputs, with human review built into the workflow.

Earlier work in cybersecurity

Assessing authentication risk

Authentication models have to work with incomplete labels and changing behavior. At SecureAuth, I combined behavioral, device, and security signals in real-time risk and anomaly-detection models, achieving over 80% precision and recall.

I also developed Bayesian risk models that reduced inference latency by 25%. This built on my earlier work at Acceptto, where I developed behavioral models and streaming feature pipelines for authentication.

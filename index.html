What I work on

Reinforcement learning

Worker incentives

I built and deployed a Bayesian reinforcement-learning system that updates worker incentives every hour across thousands of shifts.

More about this work

The system decides how to allocate incentives as staffing needs change, within a set budget. I built the training and inference pipelines, online updates, and monitoring.

I also worked on budget safeguards, testing, and controlled rollouts so we could catch problems before they affected more shifts.

Forecasting · Optimization · Experimentation

Forecasting backup staffing needs

I built understaffing forecasts and an optimizer that recommended about 31% less backup staffing in an offline comparison.

More about this work

The optimizer weighs staffing needs against replacement risk, service levels, and cost.

I also led randomized staffing and incentive experiments. In a separate staffing experiment, more shifts were filled, but net revenue fell. I presented those results and recommended against a broader rollout.

LLMs · Account prioritization

Helping account teams decide where to focus

I built an LLM-assisted workflow that brings customer calls and meeting notes together with marketplace data and forecasts.

More about this work

Information about an account was spread across conversations and operational data. I combined those sources to help teams identify retention risks, expansion opportunities, and where more support was needed.

I worked with account managers and Operations to validate the outputs and kept their review as part of the workflow.

Dynamic worker incentives

Problem, contribution & decisions

The problem: Decide how to allocate incentives as staffing needs change, while controlling spend.

My contribution: Built the decision system and owned pipelines for training, inference, online updates, monitoring, and testing.

The engineering decision: Treat budget safeguards, controlled rollouts, and incident response as part of the ML system.

Forecasting · Optimization · Experimentation

Staffing decisions with an economic reality check

Built understaffing forecasts and a profit-aware optimizer that reduced recommended backup staffing by about 31%.

Problem, contribution & decisions

The problem: Balance staffing reliability against replacement risk, service levels, and cost.

My contribution: Developed forecasting and optimization workflows and led randomized staffing and incentive experiments.

A consequential decision: In a separate staffing experiment, fill rate improved but net revenue fell. I presented the tradeoff and recommended against broad rollout.

Result: About 31% less recommended backup staffing from the optimizer.
